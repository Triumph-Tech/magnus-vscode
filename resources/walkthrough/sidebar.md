## Magnus

Triumph Tech's remote editor for Rock RMS.

Edit Mobile Apps, AI Skills, Website Themes, and Persisted Datasets directly from VS Code, either streamed from the server (Cloud mode) or pulled to a folder on your machine that VS Code, your AI tools, and the integrated terminal can all see (Local mode).

Follow along in the **Magnus** view on the activity bar.
